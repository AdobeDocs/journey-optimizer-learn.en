### What is a loyalty challenge?

So, what is a loyalty challenge? At its simplest, it's a way to turn your loyalty program into a game. You reward customers for taking specific actions — making a purchase, writing a review, referring a friend, engaging on social. A challenge sets a goal and the rules for reaching it. Customers make progress by completing tasks, and finishing those tasks — or the whole challenge — earns them rewards. And because it all runs on your Experience Platform data, progress is tracked automatically, with no custom development.

### The anatomy of a challenge

Every challenge is built from three pieces, and these are the three words to anchor on. First, the challenge itself — the overall goal and its rules: the type, the audience, the schedule, and how progress is tracked. Second, tasks — the individual actions a customer completes, like a purchase or a review. A challenge groups one or more tasks together. And third, rewards — what customers earn, delivered either at individual task milestones or when they complete the whole challenge. Challenge, tasks, rewards — if you remember nothing else, remember these three.

:::

### Ways to structure a challenge

Challenges come in a few shapes, depending on the behavior you want to drive. A Standard challenge lets customers complete any number of tasks in any order — good for flexibility. A Streak challenge asks them to repeat the same task consecutively — think buying coffee seven days in a row. A Sequential challenge requires tasks in a set order, which is great for onboarding journeys. And there's a fourth, Bring your own data, where the tasks and rewards come from your own loyalty integration — though that one has restricted availability today.

### From idea to live challenge

Here's how those pieces come together when you build one. You start by creating the challenge and picking its type. You configure its settings — audience, schedule, and rules. You give it structure by adding tasks and rewards. You design how it appears to customers with content cards, and optionally set up messaging for launch, progress, and completion. Finally, you launch — publishing the challenge and generating the journey that tracks everyone's progress. We go deep on each of these in later lessons; for now, just notice the shape of the flow.

### Key terms to remember

Let's lock in the vocabulary. A challenge is the overall goal, type, and rules. A task is an action a customer completes. A reward is what they earn, at a milestone or on completion. A content card is how the challenge shows up on a customer's device. Messaging covers the launch, in-progress, and completion moments. And the journey is the auto-generated flow that tracks progress behind the scenes. You'll see all six of these terms throughout the rest of the course.

That's the mental model. You now know what a loyalty challenge is, the three pieces it's made of, the types available, and how a challenge comes to life. Next up, we open the product and take a tour of the workspace.
